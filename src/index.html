Keyboard

Digits and . type a number (a comma also works as a decimal point). + - * / choose an operation. Enter or = shows the result. Backspace deletes the last digit. Esc clears.

The calculator works in the order you press the keys, like a pocket calculator: 2 + 3 × 4 = gives 20.

Very long answers are shown with at most 12 significant digits. The calculator still remembers the full value.
