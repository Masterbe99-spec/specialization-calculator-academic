Keyboard

Digits and . type a number. + - * / choose an operation. Enter or = shows the result. Backspace deletes the last digit. Esc clears. A comma also works as a decimal point.

Note: this calculator does the operations in the order you press them. 2 + 3 × 4 = gives 20.
