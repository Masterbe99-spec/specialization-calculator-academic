## Email

**To:** [Professor's e-mail address]
**Subject:** Specialization Project (IN.MSA.CBS.006), Team A: six short questions before the defence

Dear Professor [Professor's name],

We are the five members of Team A (the calculator application). To make sure our report and presentation meet your expectations, could you answer the questions below? Short answers are enough, and we are happy to ask them in class or in a consultation (face to face or by WEBEX) if you prefer.

1. **AI-assisted work.** Our project used an AI coding assistant for the code, the automated tests and the first drafts of the documents. Is this allowed, how should we disclose it, and does it change how the project is assessed?
2. **Report and grading.** Is there a grading rubric? Which format, length, language and citation style do you expect, and how and by when should the report be submitted?
3. **Project list.** Are the 17 items on slides 32 and 33 mandatory, or guidance (for example a class diagram, a budget and a training plan)?
4. **Defence.** Is the 10-minute limit for presentation and live demo firm, and is time for questions included in it?
5. **Cost estimate.** Do you expect a financial cost estimate (for example hours multiplied by an hourly rate), or is an effort estimate in person-hours enough?
6. **Individual contributions.** Should we hand in one team report or individual parts? How do you assess each student's contribution (for example hours, role, reflection)?

Thank you very much for your time.

Kind regards,
[Sender's name], on behalf of Team A
[Names of the five students]
